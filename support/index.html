常见问题

按住 fn 没有反应（Mac）

打开“系统设置”>“隐私与安全”，给 Mindloom 打开“麦克风”和“设备控制和数据访问”。第一次录制其他 App 或整台 Mac 的声音时，系统会询问“录屏与系统录音”。

怎样配对 iPhone 和 Mac

在 Mac 上的 Mindloom 里显示二维码，用 iPhone 上的 Mindloom 扫描。iPhone 会询问相机和本地网络的权限，两者都需要允许。两台设备通常需要在同一个局域网里。

Apple Watch 的录音保存在哪里

录音交给配对的 iPhone，在 iPhone 上转写为文字；iPhone 确认收到之前，录音保留在手表上。原件在进入 Mac 或你的 iCloud 副本之前仅存放于这台 iPhone，建议在 iPhone 上打开 iCloud 云备份，或在电脑上做加密备份。

我的内容开发者能看到吗

开发者不运行接收你内容的服务器，也没有账户、分析或崩溃上报。内容离开设备的全部路径和各自的开关见隐私政策。

怎样删除内容或断开连接

在 App 内可以删除单条录音、条目，或清空资料库；在“设置”里可以断开与 DGX Spark、腾讯会议、网络补充信息和其他 App 的连接，并删除对应的本机凭据。Mac 上的“网络活动”记录从网络补充信息发出的每一次请求，不含请求的内容，保留 90 天，可以在 App 内清除。

Common questions

Holding fn does nothing (Mac)

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and turn on Microphone and Device Control and Data Access for Mindloom. The first time you record another app or the whole Mac, the system asks about Screen & System Audio Recording.

How do I pair the iPhone and the Mac

Show the QR code in Mindloom on the Mac and scan it with Mindloom on the iPhone. The iPhone asks for Camera and Local Network permission, and both are needed. The two devices usually need to be on the same local network.

Where is an Apple Watch recording kept

The recording is handed to the paired iPhone and transcribed there; until the iPhone confirms it has the recording, it stays on the watch. Until the original reaches your Mac or your iCloud copy, it exists only on that iPhone, so we suggest turning on iCloud Backup on the iPhone, or making an encrypted backup on a computer.

Can the developer see my content

The developer runs no server that receives your content, and there are no accounts, analytics or crash reports. Every path by which content can leave a device, and the switch for each, is in the privacy policy.

How do I delete content or disconnect

In the app you can delete single recordings and items or clear the library; in Settings you can disconnect the DGX Spark, Tencent Meeting, web lookup and other apps and remove the matching local credentials. On the Mac, Network Activity records every web lookup request without its content, keeps it for 90 days, and can be cleared in the app.
