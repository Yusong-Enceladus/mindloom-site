Mindloom

口述、录音和你收进来的资料，保存在你自己的设备上。

Mac

在任意 App 里口述，文字写入当前输入位置。录制会议和系统声音。识别在这台 Mac 上进行。

iPhone

按住说话，识别在 iPhone 上完成。从分享菜单添加文字、链接、图片和文件，内容经加密送到你自己的 Mac。

Apple Watch

随手录音，交给配对的 iPhone 转写为文字。

数据在哪里

录音和转写的文字保存在你的设备上。Mindloom 没有账户，没有广告，没有第三方分析，开发者不运行接收你内容的服务器。内容离开设备的全部路径，以及每一条默认是否开启，写在隐私政策里。

需要帮助

联系方式、系统要求和常见问题在支持页。

Mindloom

Dictation, recordings and the material you collect, kept on your own devices.

Mac

Dictate into any app; the text is written at the cursor. Record meetings and system audio. Recognition runs on this Mac.

iPhone

Hold to talk; recognition runs on the iPhone. Add text, links, images and files from the share sheet; content goes over an encrypted connection to your own Mac.

Apple Watch

Record on the spot; the paired iPhone transcribes it.

Where your data lives

Recordings and their transcripts are stored on your devices. Mindloom has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics, and the developer runs no server that receives your content. Every path by which content can leave a device, and whether each is on by default, is in the privacy policy.
